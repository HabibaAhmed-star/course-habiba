Kimchi

Korea's famous fermented side dish

⏱ 30 min 📊 Medium 👤 6 servings

Kimchi is one of Korea's most iconic foods. Napa cabbage is salted and mixed with Korean chili flakes, garlic, ginger, fish sauce and fresh vegetables before being left to ferment and develop its signature spicy, tangy flavor.

How to Prepare

  1. 1

    Salt the cabbage

    Cut the cabbage into quarters and coat between the leaves with salt. Leave for about 2 hours.

  2. 2

    Rinse

    Rinse the cabbage thoroughly with cold water and drain.

  3. 3

    Make the paste

    Mix chili flakes, garlic, ginger and fish sauce into a thick seasoning paste.

  4. 4

    Add vegetables

    Mix the paste with carrots and sliced green onions.

  5. 5

    Coat the cabbage

    Massage the seasoning mixture evenly into the cabbage.

  6. 6

    Ferment

    Place in a clean jar and leave at room temperature for 1–2 days before refrigerating.

Ingredient Details

7 ingredients

🥬 Napa cabbage 1 large Fresh and crisp
🌶️ Chili flakes ½ cup Korean gochugaru
🧄 Garlic 6 cloves Freshly minced
🌱 Ginger 2 tbsp Freshly grated
🧅 Green onions 5 Sliced
🥕 Carrot 1 medium Julienned

Flavor Profile

Savory
Spice
Aromatic
Richness

💡 Chef's Tip

Allowing kimchi to ferment gives it its characteristic tangy flavor. Taste it daily to find your preferred level of fermentation.

👤 Serves 6

Delicious alongside rice, noodles, grilled meat and soups.

Bibimbap

Mixed Korean rice bowl

⏱ 45 min 📊 Medium 👤 4 servings

Bibimbap is a colorful Korean rice bowl topped with seasoned vegetables, beef and a fried egg. Everything is finished with spicy gochujang sauce and mixed together just before eating.

How to Prepare

  1. 1

    Prepare the vegetables

    Slice the vegetables and cook each separately with a little sesame oil and salt.

  2. 2

    Marinate the beef

    Mix beef with soy sauce, garlic, sesame oil and sugar.

  3. 3

    Cook the beef

    Stir-fry the beef over high heat until browned and cooked.

  4. 4

    Fry the eggs

    Fry the eggs sunny-side up while keeping the yolks soft.

  5. 5

    Assemble

    Place warm rice in bowls and arrange the vegetables and beef around the top.

  6. 6

    Serve

    Add the fried egg and gochujang, then mix everything together before eating.

Ingredient Details

7 ingredients

🍚 Rice 4 cups Short or medium grain
🥩 Beef 400g Thinly sliced
🥕 Carrot 1 Julienned
🌱 Spinach 2 cups Fresh
🍄 Mushrooms 1 cup Sliced
🥚 Eggs 4 Fried sunny-side up

Flavor Profile

Savory
Spice
Aromatic
Richness

💡 Chef's Tip

Keep each vegetable separate when cooking so every ingredient keeps its own color, texture and flavor.

👤 Serves 4

Mix everything together just before eating.

Bulgogi

Korean marinated beef

⏱ 40 min 📊 Easy 👤 4 servings

Bulgogi is made from thin slices of tender beef marinated in a sweet and savory mixture of soy sauce, sesame oil, garlic, pear and sugar. The beef is quickly grilled until caramelized.

How to Prepare

  1. 1

    Slice the beef

    Slice the beef very thinly against the grain.

  2. 2

    Make the marinade

    Mix soy sauce, grated pear, garlic, sugar and sesame oil.

  3. 3

    Marinate

    Coat the beef with the marinade and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.

  4. 4

    Heat the pan

    Heat a grill pan or skillet until very hot.

  5. 5

    Cook the beef

    Cook the beef in small batches until browned and caramelized around the edges.

  6. 6

    Serve

    Garnish with sesame seeds and green onions and serve with steamed rice.

Ingredient Details

7 ingredients

🥩 Beef 600g Thinly sliced
🥢 Soy sauce ½ cup Korean soy sauce
🍐 Asian pear ½ Finely grated
🧄 Garlic 4 cloves Minced
🌰 Sesame oil 2 tbsp Toasted sesame oil
🍬 Sugar 2 tbsp Brown sugar works well

Flavor Profile

Savory
Spice
Aromatic
Richness

💡 Chef's Tip

Freeze the beef for about 20 minutes before slicing. It makes it much easier to cut very thin pieces.

👤 Serves 4

Serve with rice, lettuce wraps and Korean side dishes.

Tteokbokki

Spicy Korean rice cakes

⏱ 25 min 📊 Easy 👤 4 servings

Tteokbokki is one of Korea's most popular street foods. Chewy cylindrical rice cakes are simmered in a sweet, spicy and savory gochujang sauce with fish cakes and green onions.

How to Prepare

  1. 1

    Prepare the sauce

    Mix gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce and sugar with water.

  2. 2

    Boil the sauce

    Bring the sauce mixture to a gentle boil.

  3. 3

    Add rice cakes

    Add the rice cakes and simmer until they become soft and chewy.

  4. 4

    Add fish cakes

    Add sliced fish cakes and continue cooking.

  5. 5

    Thicken

    Simmer until the sauce becomes thick and glossy.

  6. 6

    Serve

    Garnish with green onions and sesame seeds and serve hot.

Ingredient Details

7 ingredients

🍚 Rice cakes 500g Korean garaetteok
🌶️ Gochujang 3 tbsp Korean chili paste
🌶️ Gochugaru 1 tbsp Korean chili flakes
🥢 Soy sauce 2 tbsp For seasoning
🐟 Fish cakes 200g Sliced
🧅 Green onions 2 Sliced

Flavor Profile

Savory
Spice
Aromatic
Richness

💡 Chef's Tip

If the sauce becomes too thick, add a little water or stock while simmering to get a smooth glossy consistency.

👤 Serves 4

Best served immediately while the rice cakes are soft and chewy.

Korean Fried Chicken

Extra crispy glazed chicken

⏱ 50 min 📊 Medium 👤 4 servings

Korean fried chicken is famous for its incredibly crispy coating and sweet, spicy and sticky glaze. The chicken is fried twice to create a light and crunchy exterior before being coated in a flavorful Korean sauce.

How to Prepare

  1. 1

    Prepare the chicken

    Pat the chicken dry and season lightly with salt and pepper.

  2. 2

    Coat the chicken

    Toss the chicken pieces thoroughly in cornstarch.

  3. 3

    First fry

    Fry the chicken until cooked through and lightly golden.

  4. 4

    Make the glaze

    Simmer gochujang, honey, soy sauce and garlic until glossy and slightly thickened.

  5. 5

    Second fry

    Fry the chicken a second time until extremely crisp.

  6. 6

    Glaze & serve

    Toss the crispy chicken with the warm glaze and sprinkle with sesame seeds.

Ingredient Details

7 ingredients

🍗 Chicken wings 1 kg Fresh wings
🌾 Cornstarch 1 cup For crispy coating
🌶️ Gochujang 3 tbsp Korean chili paste
🍯 Honey 3 tbsp For sweetness
🥢 Soy sauce 2 tbsp Korean soy sauce
🧄 Garlic 4 cloves Minced

Flavor Profile

Savory
Spice
Aromatic
Richness

💡 Chef's Tip

The secret to extra crispy Korean fried chicken is frying it twice. Let the chicken rest briefly between the two fries.

👤 Serves 4

Serve immediately while the coating is still crispy.

Japchae

Korean glass noodle stir-fry

⏱ 45 min 📊 Medium 👤 4 servings

Japchae is a classic Korean noodle dish made with chewy sweet potato glass noodles, colorful vegetables, mushrooms and thinly sliced beef. Everything is tossed together with a savory sesame-soy sauce.

How to Prepare

  1. 1

    Cook the noodles

    Boil the sweet potato noodles according to the package instructions, then drain.

  2. 2

    Prepare the vegetables

    Slice the carrot, onion and mushrooms and stir-fry each until tender.

  3. 3

    Cook the spinach

    Blanch the spinach briefly, drain and season lightly.

  4. 4

    Cook the beef

    Stir-fry thinly sliced beef with soy sauce and garlic until cooked.

  5. 5

    Combine

    Toss noodles, vegetables and beef together with soy sauce and sesame oil.

  6. 6

    Serve

    Garnish with toasted sesame seeds and serve warm.

Ingredient Details

7 ingredients

🍜 Glass noodles 300g Sweet potato noodles
🥩 Beef 250g Thinly sliced
🥕 Carrot 1 medium Julienned
🧅 Onion 1 medium Thinly sliced
🍄 Mushrooms 1 cup Sliced
🌱 Spinach 2 cups Fresh spinach

Flavor Profile

Savory
Spice
Aromatic
Richness

💡 Chef's Tip

Toss the noodles with a little sesame oil after cooking so they stay separate and glossy instead of sticking together.

👤 Serves 4

Great as a main dish or as part of a Korean feast.
