Tacos al Pastor

Classic street-style tacos

⏱ 40 min📊 Medium👤 4 servings

Tacos al Pastor are one of Mexico's most beloved street foods. Thin slices of pork are marinated in a flavorful blend of achiote paste, dried chilies, and spices, then seared and served with sweet pineapple chunks on warm corn tortillas.

How to Prepare

  1. 1

    Marinate the pork

    Blend achiote paste, garlic, vinegar, orange juice, and spices into a smooth marinade. Coat the sliced pork and let rest.

  2. 2

    Cook the meat

    Sear the marinated pork slices in a hot skillet until cooked through and slightly charred at the edges.

  3. 3

    Grill the pineapple

    Sear fresh pineapple slices in the same skillet until caramelised, then chop into small pieces.

  4. 4

    Warm the tortillas

    Heat corn tortillas on a warm skillet until soft and pliable.

  5. 5

    Assemble the tacos

    Place a portion of meat on each tortilla. Top with grilled pineapple, diced onions, and fresh cilantro.

  6. 6

    Serve

    Serve immediately with fresh lime wedges and your favorite Mexican salsa.

Ingredient Details

8 ingredients

🥩Pork shoulder500gThinly sliced
🌶️Achiote paste2 tbspTraditional spice blend
🍊Orange juice100mlFreshly squeezed
🍍Pineapple150gFresh slices
🌽Corn tortillas8 smallWarm
🧅White onion1 mediumFinely chopped
🌿Cilantro1 handfulFreshly chopped
🍋Limes2Cut into wedges

Flavor Profile

Savory
Spice
Aromatic
Richness

💡 Chef's Tip

Sear the pork on high heat in batches to get charred edges without overcrowding the pan. The sweetness of roasted pineapple perfectly balances the savory spice.

👤Serves 4

Makes 8 street-style tacos. Best enjoyed hot!

Guacamole

Fresh avocado dip

⏱ 15 min📊 Easy👤 4 servings

Guacamole is an ancient Mexican avocado-based dip dating back to the Aztecs. Made with ripe creamy avocados, freshly squeezed lime juice, cilantro, onions, and spicy jalapeños, it is light, fresh, and universally adored.

How to Prepare

  1. 1

    Prep the avocados

    Cut the avocados in half, remove the pits, and scoop the flesh into a medium bowl or molcajete.

  2. 2

    Mash gently

    Use a fork or potato masher to coarse-mash the avocados, keeping some chunky texture.

  3. 3

    Add aromatics

    Fold in the finely diced onion, chopped jalapeño, tomato, and fresh cilantro.

  4. 4

    Season

    Squeeze fresh lime juice over the mixture and sprinkle with sea salt.

  5. 5

    Mix and taste

    Stir everything together gently. Taste and adjust lime juice or salt as desired.

  6. 6

    Serve

    Serve immediately with warm, crispy tortilla chips.

Ingredient Details

7 ingredients

🥑Avocados3 ripeHass avocados preferred
🍋Lime juice2 tbspFreshly squeezed
🧅Red onion½ mediumFinely diced
🍅Roma tomato1 mediumDiced, seeds removed
🌶️Jalapeño1Finely chopped
🌿Cilantro½ cupChopped fresh
🧂Sea salt1 tspTo taste

Flavor Profile

Savory
Spice
Aromatic
Richness

💡 Chef's Tip

Always use fresh lime juice to prevent the avocado from browning and to provide that essential bright, zesty contrast to the rich avocado fats.

👤Serves 4

Great as an appetizer or a flavorful topping for tacos.

Enchiladas Rojas

Rolled stuffed tortillas

⏱ 45 min📊 Medium👤 4 servings

Enchiladas Rojas consist of corn tortillas dipped in a rich, mild red chili sauce, stuffed with seasoned chicken, rolled up, and baked under a blanket of melted cheese and fresh crema.

How to Prepare

  1. 1

    Make the red sauce

    Simmer dried guajillo chilies, garlic, and tomatoes, then blend with spices until smooth and strainer-smooth.

  2. 2

    Prepare the filling

    Shred cooked chicken breast and toss with a little salt, cumin, and onion powder.

  3. 3

    Soften tortillas

    Lightly fry each corn tortilla in oil for 5 seconds per side until soft and flexible.

  4. 4

    Fill and roll

    Dip each tortilla into warm red sauce, place chicken filling inside, and roll tightly into a baking dish.

  5. 5

    Bake

    Pour remaining sauce over the top, cover generously with cheese, and bake at 190°C for 20 minutes.

  6. 6

    Garnish & serve

    Top with Mexican crema, crumbled queso fresco, and fresh cilantro before serving.

Ingredient Details

8 ingredients

🌽Corn tortillas8Yellow corn
🍗Shredded chicken400gCooked breast or thigh
🌶️Guajillo chilies4 driedDeseeded for sauce
🍅Tomatoes3 mediumBoiled for sauce
🧄Garlic3 clovesPeeled
🧀Monterey Jack cheese200gShredded
🥛Mexican crema½ cupFor drizzling
🌿Cilantro1 handfulFresh garnish

Flavor Profile

Savory
Spice
Aromatic
Richness

💡 Chef's Tip

Quickly flash-frying tortillas in oil prevents them from tearing or becoming soggy when dipped into the hot chili sauce.

👤Serves 4

Serve warm alongside Mexican red rice and refried beans.

Cheese Quesadillas

Melted cheese tortillas

⏱ 20 min📊 Easy👤 2 servings

A staple Mexican comfort meal, quesadillas are simple yet deeply satisfying. Tortillas are filled with generous amounts of melting cheese, folded over, and toasted on a flat skillet until golden and bubbly.

How to Prepare

  1. 1

    Heat the skillet

    Preheat a dry skillet or comal over medium-low heat.

  2. 2

    Add cheese

    Place a tortilla on the hot skillet and spread cheese evenly over one half.

  3. 3

    Fold

    Fold the tortilla in half over the cheese to form a half-moon shape.

  4. 4

    Toast

    Cook for 2-3 minutes until the bottom is crisp and golden, then flip and cook the other side until cheese melts completely.

  5. 5

    Slice

    Remove from heat, let cool for one minute, then slice into triangular wedges.

  6. 6

    Serve

    Serve hot with fresh salsa, guacamole, and sour cream on the side.

Ingredient Details

5 ingredients

🫓Tortillas4 largeFlour or corn
🧀Oaxaca cheese250gOr Chihuahua / Monterey Jack
🧈Butter1 tbspOptional for extra crispness
🥑Guacamole½ cupFor serving
🌶️Salsa roja½ cupFor dipping

Flavor Profile

Savory
Spice
Aromatic
Richness

💡 Chef's Tip

Keep the flame on medium-low. This allows the cheese inside to melt completely before the outside of the tortilla burns.

👤Serves 2

Perfect for a quick snack or simple dinner.

Mexican Churros

Crispy cinnamon dessert

⏱ 35 min📊 Medium👤 4 servings

Churros are popular fried dough pastries cooked until crispy and golden, then dusted generously with cinnamon sugar. In Mexico, they are traditionally enjoyed hot with a cup of rich chocolate dipping sauce.

How to Prepare

  1. 1

    Make the dough

    Bring water, butter, and sugar to a boil. Remove from heat, stir in flour until a dough forms, then beat in eggs one at a time.

  2. 2

    Prepare cinnamon sugar

    Mix sugar and ground cinnamon together in a shallow dish for coating.

  3. 3

    Heat oil

    Heat vegetable oil in a deep pot to 180°C (350°F).

  4. 4

    Pipe the dough

    Spoon dough into a piping bag with a star tip. Pipe 10cm strips directly into hot oil, cutting with kitchen shears.

  5. 5

    Fry

    Fry for 2 minutes per side until deep golden brown. Drain briefly on paper towels.

  6. 6

    Coat & serve

    Roll hot churros immediately in cinnamon sugar. Serve warm with warm Mexican chocolate sauce.

Ingredient Details

8 ingredients

🌾All-purpose flour125gSifted
💧Water250mlClean water
🧈Butter60gUnsalted
🥚Eggs2 largeAt room temperature
🍬Sugar100gGranulated
🍂Cinnamon1 tbspGround cinnamon
🍫Dark chocolate100gFor dipping sauce
🌻Vegetable oil1LFor deep frying

Flavor Profile

Savory
Spice
Aromatic
Richness

💡 Chef's Tip

Always use a star-shaped piping tip! The ridges help the churros cook evenly and hold onto maximum cinnamon-sugar goodness.

👤Serves 4

Best enjoyed freshly made while hot and crispy!

Chilaquiles

Traditional Mexican breakfast

⏱ 25 min📊 Medium👤 2 servings

Chilaquiles are a traditional Mexican breakfast dish featuring crispy tortilla chips simmered briefly in warm red or green salsa until slightly softened yet crunchy, topped with eggs, cheese, and crema.

How to Prepare

  1. 1

    Prepare tortillas

    Cut corn tortillas into triangles and fry in hot oil until crispy and golden brown. Drain on paper towels.

  2. 2

    Warm salsa

    Pour green (tomatillo) or red salsa into a wide skillet and bring to a gentle simmer.

  3. 3

    Toss chips

    Add fried tortilla chips to the simmering salsa and gently fold to coat evenly for 1-2 minutes.

  4. 4

    Fry eggs

    In a separate pan, fry two eggs sunny-side up until whites are set but yolks remain runny.

  5. 5

    Plate

    Transfer salsa-coated chips to plates and place fried eggs on top.

  6. 6

    Garnish & serve

    Drizzle with crema, sprinkle with queso fresco, sliced red onions, and fresh cilantro leaves.

Ingredient Details

8 ingredients

🌽Corn tortillas8 smallCut into triangles
🌶️Mexican Salsa1½ cupsRed or green salsa
🥚Eggs2 largeFried sunny-side up
🧀Queso fresco60gCrumbled
🥛Mexican crema3 tbspFor drizzle
🧅Red onion¼ mediumThinly sliced
🌿Cilantro1 tbspFresh leaves
🥑Avocado½Sliced for topping

💡 Chef's Tip
